## External validation still needed

The Music Accessibility flow was reworked on 19 November 2026 without a Music session: song rows are scored (title, artist, album, duration, link, selection) instead of matched by exact title, searched across tables and then any element naming the song, scrolled into view, and selected by AX or click with a polled check; Play Next, Add to Queue and Remove try the Song menu, AXShowMenu and the context menu in turn; Play Now first sends Music's next-track script command (the new request is first in the queue), then a double-click, then Return, reading the queue back after each. Every wait is an async bounded poll. The background refresh reads Playing Next on startup, on track change and every 10 s (3 s while unverified), opens the panel once per Music process, and reports a reason only when a read fails or the panel stays hidden. The row scoring, read policy and focus escalation have unit tests (`SongRowMatcherTests`, `QueueReadPolicyTests`, `ActivationFallbackTests`); the AX behavior itself, including Music's search results layout, `AXScrollToVisible`, `AXShowMenu` and Return-to-play, is unverified until run against a real Music.app.

Music control is focus-neutral first. Each queue operation runs through `ActivationFallback` in up to three routes: Accessibility actions and attribute writes on the background app (menu-bar and View menu presses, AXShowMenu, AXSelectedRows/AXSelected/AXFocused writes), then mouse and key events posted to Music's process with `CGEvent.postToPid`, and only then Music is activated, the HID-posted version of the step runs, and the previously frontmost app is re-activated even when the step fails (unless the user already switched elsewhere). Queue commands no longer bring Music forward beforehand, and `music://` links are opened with `activates = false`. These paths can still activate Music, each only after the quieter routes failed: selecting or focusing a row by click, a Song, AX or context menu item that Music refuses to press through AX, Play Now by double-click or Return, dragging a queued request, pressing View > Show Playing Next (including the once-per-process refresh) or Show All when the AX press fails, and finding Music's window when it exposes none to Accessibility (typed error `music_window_unavailable`). Whether `postToPid` clicks, Return and drags are honored by a backgrounded Music, whether Music accepts AXPress on its menu items and rows while inactive, whether `music://` and `reveal` leave Music in the background, whether a backgrounded Music exposes its window tree, and whether next-track plays the freshly inserted request all remain unverified without a real Music session.
